# Budget Allocation Frameworks for E-Commerce Ads

Detailed budget allocation strategies for e-commerce stores at different budget levels.

## Micro Budget: ₹5,000-10,000/month ($60-120)

### Phase 1: Proof of Concept (Weeks 1-2)

| Allocation | Amount | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Meta prospecting | 100% | Build pixel data, find winning creative |
| Retargeting | 0% | Not enough traffic yet |

**Goal:** Get first 3-5 sales, identify winning ad creative.

### Phase 2: Optimize (Weeks 3-4)

| Allocation | Amount | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Meta prospecting | 75% | Scale winning audiences |
| Retargeting | 25% | Convert warm visitors |

**Goal:** Achieve target ROAS, prove the model works.

### Rules at Micro Budget
- Run only 1 campaign at a time
- Test maximum 3 ad creatives per ad set
- Don't split across platforms
- Need minimum ₹200/day to give Meta enough data
- Kill underperformers fast (after 500 impressions, no clicks)

---

## Small Budget: ₹10,000-25,000/month ($120-300)

### Allocation

| Allocation | Amount | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Meta prospecting | 60% | Primary acquisition channel |
| Meta retargeting | 20% | Convert visitors and cart abandoners |
| Google Shopping | 20% | Capture high-intent search traffic |

### Campaign Structure

**Meta (80% of budget):**
- 1 Advantage+ Shopping campaign (prospecting)
- 1 Retargeting campaign (website visitors 7-14 days)
- 3-5 ad creatives per ad set

**Google Shopping (20% of budget):**
- 1 Standard Shopping campaign
- Focus on top 5-10 products
- Set target ROAS bidding

---

## Medium Budget: ₹25,000-75,000/month ($300-900)

### Allocation

| Allocation | Amount | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Meta prospecting | 45% | Broad + interest audiences |
| Meta retargeting | 15% | Website visitors, cart abandoners |
| Google Shopping | 25% | Product listing ads |
| Google Search | 10% | Brand + category keywords |
| Testing | 5% | New platforms, creative tests |

### Additional Tactics
- Test Google Performance Max campaigns
- Introduce lookalike audiences on Meta
- Start A/B testing landing pages
- Test Instagram Reels placement separately

---

## Budget Scaling Rules

### When to Increase Budget
- ROAS consistently above target for 7+ days
- Frequency below 2.0 (not showing same ad too often)
- CPA stable or decreasing

### How to Increase Budget
- Increase by maximum 20-30% at a time
- Wait 3-5 days after each increase
- Monitor ROAS closely for 48 hours after increase
- If ROAS drops, revert and try again in a week

### When NOT to Increase Budget
- ROAS is below breakeven
- You haven't optimized creative in 2+ weeks
- Product page conversion rate is below 1%
- You're running out of stock on hero products

## Platform-Specific Budget Minimums

### Meta Ads
- **Absolute minimum:** ₹200/day ($2.50)
- **Recommended minimum:** ₹500/day ($6) for meaningful data
- **Learning phase:** Needs ~50 conversions/week per ad set for optimal delivery
- **Workaround for small budgets:** Use "Add to Cart" as conversion event instead of "Purchase" to get more data points

### Google Shopping
- **Absolute minimum:** ₹300/day ($4)
- **Recommended minimum:** ₹800/day ($10) for competitive categories
- **Smart Shopping:** Needs 15+ conversions in 30 days to optimize effectively
- **Workaround for small budgets:** Start with manual CPC, switch to Smart bidding after 30+ conversions

### Google Search
- **Absolute minimum:** ₹500/day ($6)
- **Recommended minimum:** ₹1,000/day ($12)
- **Use only for:** Brand terms and high-intent long-tail keywords at small budgets

## Seasonal Budget Adjustments (India Market)

| Period | Budget Multiplier | Reason |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| Diwali season (Oct-Nov) | 2-3x | Highest purchase intent |
| End of year sales (Dec) | 1.5-2x | Holiday gifting |
| Republic Day / Independence Day | 1.3-1.5x | Sale events |
| Valentine's Day / Rakhi | 1.5x (if relevant) | Gifting occasions |
| March-April | 0.8x | Post-festive slowdown |
| Prime Day / Big Billion Days | 1.5x | Marketplace FOMO drives all e-commerce |

### Festive Season Strategy
1. Start building audiences 4-6 weeks before major festivals
2. Increase budget 2 weeks before the festival
3. Peak spend during festival week
4. Retarget festival visitors for 2-3 weeks after
5. Use festival-specific creatives and offers
