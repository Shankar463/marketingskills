## Step 1: Pre-Launch Checklist

Before spending a single rupee on ads, make sure these are in place:

- [ ] **Website is working** — All product pages load, images are clear, prices are visible
- [ ] **Payment gateway is live** — UPI, cards, COD (Cash on Delivery is critical for India)
- [ ] **Shipping policy is clear** — Delivery timelines, charges, and free-shipping thresholds
- [ ] **Return policy exists** — Even a simple 7-day return policy builds trust
- [ ] **Product images are high quality** — At least 3 images per product (on model, flat lay, detail)
- [ ] **Product descriptions are complete** — Size, material, care instructions, fit guide
- [ ] **Trust signals are present** — Reviews, ratings, secure payment badges, "100% genuine" messaging
- [ ] **Meta Pixel installed** — Go to Meta Business Suite → Events Manager → Set up Pixel on your site
- [ ] **Google Analytics 4 (GA4) installed** — Track visitors, add-to-carts, and purchases
- [ ] **WhatsApp button added** — Indian customers prefer WhatsApp for queries

## Step 2: Calculate Your Breakeven ROAS

You must know your breakeven ROAS before running ads. This tells you the **minimum return** you need to not lose money.

### Formula

```
Breakeven ROAS = 1 ÷ Profit Margin %
```

### Calculate for Threadzu

Fill in your numbers:

| Metric | Your Number | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Average Selling Price (ASP) | ₹_____ | ₹800 |
| Cost of Goods (COGS) | ₹_____ | ₹350 |
| Shipping Cost per Order | ₹_____ | ₹60 |
| Payment Gateway Fee (~2%) | ₹_____ | ₹16 |
| **Profit per Order** | ₹_____ | **₹374** |
| **Profit Margin %** | ____% | **46.7%** |
| **Breakeven ROAS** | ___x | **2.14x** |

### Target ROAS

Your **target ROAS should be 1.5× your breakeven** to account for returns, refunds, and overhead:

```
If Breakeven ROAS = 2.14x
→ Target ROAS = 2.14 × 1.5 = 3.2x (round to 3x)
```

**For fashion/apparel in India, aim for 3–5x ROAS.**

## Step 3: Select Hero Products to Advertise

With a small budget, **do NOT advertise your entire catalog**. Pick 1–3 hero products.

### Product Selection Criteria for Threadzu

| Factor | Best for Ads ✅ | Avoid for Ads ❌ |
|--------|----------------|------------------|
| **Price** | ₹500–₹2,000 range | Below ₹200 (margins too thin) |
| **Margin** | >40% gross margin | <25% margin |
| **Visual appeal** | Looks great on a model | Hard to photograph |
| **Reviews** | Has 5+ positive reviews | Zero reviews |
| **Uniqueness** | Distinctive designs | Generic commodity items |
| **Impulse buy** | Easy "yes" purchase | Needs long deliberation |

1. **Pick 1 hero product** — your highest margin + best-looking + best-reviewed item
2. **Create a compelling offer** — "Free shipping on orders above ₹499" or "Buy 2, Get 10% off"
3. **Drive ALL ad traffic to that product page** — not your homepage
4. **Use other products for retargeting** — show browsing visitors your wider catalog

## Step 4: Choose Your Ad Platform

### Recommendation for Threadzu: **Start with Meta Ads (Instagram + Facebook)**

Why Meta is best for a fashion/apparel store:

- ✅ Instagram is where fashion buyers discover brands
- ✅ Visual ad formats (Reels, carousel, Stories) showcase clothing perfectly
- ✅ Lowest minimum budget (₹200/day)
- ✅ Advantage+ Shopping campaigns handle targeting automatically
- ✅ Huge fashion-interested audience in India

```
Threadzu.in — Fashion / Apparel
├── Products are visually appealing? → YES ✅
├── Target audience is on Instagram? → YES ✅ (18-35 age group)
├── Budget is small? → YES ✅
└── DECISION → Start with Meta (Instagram + Facebook)
```

| Setting | Value |

| **Gender** | Based on your product line |

## Step 7: Create Ad Creatives

### Ad Creative Checklist for Fashion/Apparel

#### Photo Ads (minimum 3 variations)

- [ ] **Lifestyle shot** — Product on a model in a real-world setting
- [ ] **Product shot** — Clean, well-lit product image with offer text overlay
- [ ] **Carousel** — Multiple products or multiple angles of hero product

#### Video/Reels Ads (highest performing for fashion)

- [ ] **Styling Reel** — 15 seconds showing how to style the product
- [ ] **Unboxing Reel** — Packaging reveal + product close-up
- [ ] **Before/After or Transformation** — Shows the product's impact

### Ad Copy Templates for Threadzu

**Template 1 — Lifestyle**
```
Headline: Your Next Favorite [Product Type] 🧵
Primary Text: Designed for [audience]. Made with [material/quality].
Shop now at Threadzu — Free shipping on orders above ₹499.
CTA: Shop Now
```

**Template 2 — Offer-Focused**
```
Headline: [Product Name] — Now ₹[Price]
Primary Text: 🔥 Limited time: Buy 2, save 10%.
Premium quality. Easy returns. COD available.
CTA: Shop Now
```

**Template 3 — Social Proof**
```
Headline: [X]+ Happy Customers Love This
Primary Text: "Best quality I've found online" — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
See why [Product] is Threadzu's #1 bestseller.
CTA: Shop Now
```

### Creative Best Practices

1. **Show the product in the first 3 seconds** of any video
2. **Include the price** if it's competitive
3. **UGC (User Generated Content) style** beats polished studio content at small budgets
4. **Use Indian context** — festivals, seasons, local styling cues
5. **Always include a CTA** — "Shop Now" or "Order Today"

## Step 9: Optimization Playbook

Use this decision tree whenever ROAS is below target:

### Problem → Diagnosis → Fix

**Getting clicks but no sales?**
- → Check product page: Is it clear? Are images good? Is the price visible?
- → Check checkout: Is it easy? Is COD available?
- → Check shipping cost: Hidden costs kill conversions
- → Add trust signals: Reviews, "genuine product" badge, return policy
- → Try a better offer: Free shipping, discount, bundle

**Not getting clicks on ads?**
- → Improve ad creative: Test a Reel or lifestyle image
- → Make the offer more compelling in the ad copy
- → Check targeting: Too narrow? Too irrelevant?
- → Test a different hero product

**High CPM (expensive impressions)?**
- → Broaden your targeting
- → Try different placements (Reels tend to be cheaper)
- → Improve ad quality score with higher-engagement creatives

**CPA (cost per sale) too high?**
- → Focus on higher-AOV products or create bundles
- → Tighten retargeting window to 3–7 days
- → Pause all underperforming ad sets

## Step 10: Zero-Budget & Low-Cost Tactics

Complement your paid ads with these free strategies:

### 📱 Organic Instagram (Free)
- Post 4–5 times per week: product shots, styling tips, behind-the-scenes
- Create Reels — they get 3–5x more reach than static posts
- Use 20–25 relevant hashtags per post
- Engage daily: Comment on potential customers' posts

### 💬 WhatsApp Marketing (Free / Low-Cost)
- Add WhatsApp button to threadzu.in
- Create broadcast list of past customers
- Send new arrival and exclusive offers
- Use WhatsApp Business catalog feature

### 🤝 Influencer Barter (Product Cost Only)
- Find 3–5 micro-influencers (1K–10K followers) in fashion/lifestyle
- Offer free products in exchange for content (Reels + Stories)
- Repurpose their content as ad creative (with permission)

### 📧 Email Marketing (Free)
- Add email popup: "Get 10% off your first order"
- Set up 3-email abandoned cart sequence
- Send weekly newsletter with new arrivals and offers

### 🔍 SEO (Free, Long-Term)
- Optimize product titles for search: "Buy [product type] online India"
- Write category page descriptions
- Start a blog with styling tips and lookbooks

## Step 11: Common Mistakes to Avoid

| ❌ Mistake | 💡 Do This Instead |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Splitting ₹5K across 5 campaigns | Focus on 1–2 campaigns max |
| Changing ads every day | Wait 5 days / 50+ clicks before judging |
| Targeting too narrow | Start broad, let algorithm optimize |
| Sending traffic to homepage | Send to the specific product page |
| No retargeting | Set up retargeting from Week 2 |
| Ignoring product page quality | Optimize your page first |
| No urgency or offer | Always have a compelling reason to buy now |
| Only boosting Instagram posts | Use Ads Manager for real campaigns |
| Not having COD | COD is expected for new Indian D2C brands |
