# Design Analysis Frameworks

Detailed frameworks for product design evaluation, competitor analysis, and ad-readiness assessment.

## Design Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to objectively evaluate each design. Score 1-5 for each dimension.

### Visual Impact (Weight: 30%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Immediately eye-catching, unique, professional quality |
| 4 | Strong visual appeal, stands out from most competitors |
| 3 | Decent design, but doesn't particularly stand out |
| 2 | Below average, looks amateur or generic |
| 1 | Poor quality, would scroll past without noticing |

### Market Relevance (Weight: 25%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Perfectly targets the niche, high demand signals |
| 4 | Strong niche fit, clear audience appeal |
| 3 | Somewhat relevant, could appeal to the audience |
| 2 | Weak connection to target market |
| 1 | No clear market fit or audience appeal |

### Print Quality (Weight: 20%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Print-ready at 300+ DPI, colors print-accurate, tested |
| 4 | High resolution, good color choices for print |
| 3 | Adequate resolution, minor color concerns |
| 2 | Low resolution or problematic colors for print |
| 1 | Not print-ready, will look bad on product |

### Uniqueness (Weight: 15%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Completely original concept and execution |
| 4 | Unique angle on a familiar theme |
| 3 | Somewhat original, but similar designs exist |
| 2 | Very similar to common designs in the market |
| 1 | Generic, indistinguishable from competitors |

### Ad Potential (Weight: 10%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Perfect for ads — clear, emotional, scroll-stopping |
| 4 | Strong ad potential with good mockups |
| 3 | Could work in ads with better presentation |
| 2 | Weak ad potential, unlikely to drive clicks |
| 1 | Not suitable for paid advertising |

### Scoring Guide

- **4.0-5.0**: Ad-ready. Launch and scale.
- **3.0-3.9**: Has potential. Improve before investing in ads.
- **2.0-2.9**: Needs significant work. Don't run ads on these.
- **1.0-1.9**: Retire or completely redesign.

## Meta Ad-Readiness Checklist (Detailed)

### Product Readiness

- [ ] Minimum 5 strong designs in the store
- [ ] Each design has a professional mockup
- [ ] Product pages have compelling descriptions
- [ ] Pricing is competitive (research 10+ competitors)
- [ ] Shipping options are clear and reasonable
- [ ] Return/exchange policy is published
- [ ] Store looks professional and trustworthy

### Creative Readiness

- [ ] At least 3 ad images per design (different angles/mockups)
- [ ] Lifestyle photos or realistic mockups (not flat designs)
- [ ] Images are 1080x1080 (square) and 1080x1920 (story) minimum
- [ ] Designs are clear and readable at mobile thumbnail size
- [ ] Text overlay is under 20% of image area
- [ ] Brand is visible but not overpowering

### Targeting Readiness

- [ ] Target audience clearly defined (demographics + interests)
- [ ] Audience size is between 500K-5M (Meta sweet spot)
- [ ] Geographic targeting is set (start narrow, expand later)
- [ ] Lookalike audiences prepared (if you have customer data)
- [ ] Interest targeting researched (related brands, pages, behaviors)

### Budget Readiness

- [ ] Test budget: minimum $20-50/day for 5-7 days per design
- [ ] Enough budget to test at least 3-5 designs (total test budget: $300-1,750)
- [ ] Prepared to wait 3-5 days before making decisions
- [ ] Break-even ROAS calculated

## Print-on-Demand Design Specifications

### Common Product Specs

| Product | Print Area | Recommended Resolution | File Format |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|-------------|
| T-shirt (front) | 12" x 16" | 3600 x 4800 px (300 DPI) | PNG (transparent bg) |
| T-shirt (pocket) | 3.5" x 3.5" | 1050 x 1050 px | PNG |
| Hoodie (front) | 12" x 12" | 3600 x 3600 px | PNG |
| Mug (wrap) | 9" x 3.5" | 2700 x 1050 px | PNG |
| Poster (18x24) | 18" x 24" | 5400 x 7200 px | PNG/PDF |
| Phone case | 6" x 10" | 1800 x 3000 px | PNG |
| Tote bag | 14" x 16" | 4200 x 4800 px | PNG |

### Color Guidelines for Print

- **CMYK awareness**: Designs appear duller in print than on screen
- **Dark garments**: Use DTG or screen print white underbase
- **Light garments**: Most colors translate well
- **Neon/bright colors**: Will not print as vibrant as screen display
- **Always order samples** before selling

### File Preparation Checklist

- [ ] 300 DPI minimum resolution
- [ ] Transparent background (PNG)
- [ ] CMYK color mode for professional printing
- [ ] RGB for DTG and most POD platforms
- [ ] Bleed area included if required
- [ ] Text converted to outlines
- [ ] No copyrighted elements
